# MATERI 1: ADVANCED NARRATIVE TEXT (ALEGORI & SASTRA TINGKAT LANJUT)

### 1. Definisi Teoretis & Fungsi Komunikatif
- **Definisi:** Advanced Narrative Text adalah karya fiksi sastra tingkat lanjut yang mengeksplorasi kedalaman psikologis karakter, dilema moral yang ambigu, serta menggunakan bahasa simbolik (*allegory & symbolism*) untuk mengkritik realitas modern atau menggugah perenungan eksistensial pembaca.
- **Fungsi Sosial (*Social Function*):** *To explore existential, philosophical, and moral dilemmas through multi-layered narration.* Tidak hanya mengisahkan peristiwa lahiriah, melainkan benturan nilai kemanusiaan abadi melawan arus zaman.

### 2. Unsur-Unsur Struktur Teks (*Generic Structure & Text Elements*)
1. **Atmospheric Orientation (Latar Batin & Simbolik):** Membangun suasana emosional yang pekat melalui citraan sensorik arsitektur kuno, aroma khas, dan nuansa psikologis tokoh (Paragraf 1).
2. **Multi-layered Complication (Benturan Paradigma):** Konflik batin atau benturan ideologi (misalnya efisiensi teknologi digital dingin versus ketulusan karya tangan manusia) (Paragraf 2).
3. **Epiphany / Turning Point (Momen Pencerahan Batin):** Momen hening ketika tokoh menyadari hakikat kebenaran yang lebih dalam melalui dialog atau benda pusaka (Paragraf 3).
4. **Reflective Open Resolution (Resolusi Kontemplatif):** Akhir cerita yang memberi ruang refleksi bagi pembaca tanpa menyederhanakan masalah menjadi hitam-putih (Paragraf 4).

### 3. Contoh Teks yang Relevan (Model Text Teranotasi)
**Judul:** *The Memory Weaver of Kota Lama* (Asian Writers Review, CEFR B2)
- **[Paragraf 1 - Orientation]:** *Deep within the labyrinthine alleyways of Semarang’s Old Town, flanked by Dutch colonial archways whose plaster flaked like desiccated parchment, sat Master Sastro. For over six decades, he had practiced the vanishing art of hand-binding antique manuscripts, stitching fragile leather covers with waxed hemp thread...*
- **[Paragraf 2 - Complication]:** *One sweltering afternoon, a young software developer named Nadia entered his workshop carrying an ultrathin holographic scanner. Tasked by a municipal archive consortium to digitize historical artifacts, she viewed Sastro’s ancient volumes merely as unindexed data sets awaiting conversion into high-density neural vectors. "Bapak," Nadia remarked politely, "my machine can preserve your entire life's work in less than twelve minutes..."*
- **[Paragraf 3 - Epiphany]:** *Sastro paused, setting down his polished bone folder. He picked up a sun-bleached Javanese chronicle transcribed in golden botanical ink and gently held it near her ear. "Listen closely, young lady," he whispered gently. "A machine can capture the shape of these letters with ruthless optical precision, but can it register the trembling hand of the poet who composed these verses by candlelight during the famine of 1845? Can your server transmit the scent of rain trapped between these dried leaves?"*
- **[Paragraf 4 - Resolution]:** *Nadia froze, the hum of her cooling fan suddenly sounding intrusive amidst the solemn silence of the workshop. For the first time, she looked beyond the optical surface of the document, recognizing that cultural preservation is not merely an algorithmic calculation of pixels, but a sacred transmission of human vulnerability across generations.*

### 4. Grammar & Tata Bahasa Fokus
- **Participle Clauses:** *Tasked by a municipal archive consortium, Nadia viewed the books as data sets.*
- **Cleft Sentences:** *It was not the optical scanner, but the poet's sincere devotion that gave soul to the text.*

### 5. Pronunciation Guide & Speaking Tips
- **Labyrinthine** (/ˌlæbəˈrɪnθaɪn/ - lab-uh-**RIN**-thyne): Berliku-liku rumit bagai labirin.
- **Ephemeral** (/ɪˈfemərəl/ - ih-**FEM**-er-uhl): Fana / hanya berlangsung sekejap.
- **Vulnerability** (/ˌvʌlnərəˈbɪləti/ - vul-ner-uh-**BIL**-i-tee): Kerapuhan nurani & kepekaan kemanusiaan.

# MATERI 2: ARGUMENT & STATING AGREEMENT (WACANA ARGUMENTASI & DIPLOMASI)

### 1. Definisi Teoretis & Fungsi Komunikatif
- **Definisi:** Teks argumentasi tingkat lanjut menuntut penguasaan struktur penalaran ilmiah (*Claim, Evidence, Warrant, Concession*) serta retorika diplomatis santun dalam menyatakan persetujuan formal dan penyelarasan sikap (*Stating Agreement*) di forum akademik internasional.
- **Fungsi Sosial (*Social Function*):** *To formulate rigorous academic arguments with evidentiary backing and articulate diplomatic alignment in high-stakes deliberative contexts.*

### 2. Unsur-Unsur Struktur Teks (*Generic Structure & Text Elements*)
1. **Claim (Pernyataan Klaim):** Pernyataan pendirian utama yang didukung data dan penalaran (*"Decarbonization mandates equitable financial solidarity between nations"*).
2. **Evidence & Warrant (Bukti & Prinsip Pembenar):** Fakta empiris atau prinsip hukum internasional yang menghubungkan klaim dengan data.
3. **Concession (Pengakuan Konsesif):** Mengakui validitas sebagian poin lawan sebelum memperkuat klaim mandiri (*"Notwithstanding the high upfront capital cost of renewables,..."*).
4. **Diplomatic Agreement (Persetujuan Diplomatik):** Penyelarasan sikap delegasi secara resmi dan berwibawa (*"Our delegation aligns itself entirely with that assessment; your premise is both just and indispensable"*).

### 3. Contoh Wacana yang Relevan (Model Dialogue Teranotasi)
**Konteks:** *Model United Nations Climate Deliberation (International Youth Diplomacy)*
- **[Giliran 1 - Claim & Concession]:** *Delegate of Indonesia: "Honorable Chair, notwithstanding the urgent imperative of global decarbonization, developing economies cannot sacrifice basic rural livelihoods. The principle of Common But Differentiated Responsibilities dictates that industrialized nations must provide substantive green technology transfers and concessional financing."*
- **[Giliran 2 - Diplomatic Agreement]:** *Delegate of Germany: "Our delegation aligns itself entirely with that assessment. The premise articulated by the distinguished delegate of Indonesia is both equitable and indispensable. Sustainable decarbonization can only succeed when financial solidarity accompanies ecological mandates."*
- **[Giliran 3 - Consensus]:** *Delegate of Indonesia: "We warmly welcome this consensus and invite all member states to co-sponsor the clean energy equity framework."*

### 4. Grammar & Tata Bahasa Fokus
- **Concessive Connectors:**
  - *Notwithstanding the high capital costs, renewable microgrids stabilize farming economies.*
  - *The proposal, albeit ambitious, offers the only viable roadmap toward net-zero emissions.*
- **Diplomatic Stance Markers:**
  - *Our delegation aligns itself entirely with your core assessment.*

### 5. Pronunciation Guide & Speaking Tips
- **Notwithstanding** (/ˌnɒtwɪθˈstændɪŋ/ - not-with-**STAN**-ding): Meskipun demikian / terlepas dari.
- **Concessional** (/kənˈseʃənl/ - kuhn-**SESH**-uh-nuhl): Bersifat lunak / berbunga sangat rendah.
- **Indispensable** (/ˌɪndɪˈspensəbl/ - in-di-**SPEN**-suh-bl): Sangat mutlak / tak dapat ditiadakan.
- **Equitable** (/ˈekwɪtəbl/ - **EK**-wi-tuh-bl): Berkeadilan / merata sesuai hak.

# MATERI 3: DISCUSSION TEXT (TEKS DISKUSI WACANA BERIMBANG)

### 1. Definisi Teoretis & Fungsi Komunikatif
- **Definisi:** Discussion Text adalah teks argumentatif yang membahas suatu isu kontroversial atau dilematis dari setidaknya dua sudut pandang yang berlawanan (*Arguments For / Pro vs Arguments Against / Contra*) sebelum menyajikan kesimpulan sintesis netral.
- **Fungsi Sosial (*Social Function*):** *To present differing viewpoints on an issue before formulating a balanced conclusion.* Penulis bertindak sebagai penengah yang adil dan objektif (*neutral arbiter*).

### 2. Unsur-Unsur Struktur Teks (*Generic Structure & Text Elements*)
1. **Statement of Issue (Rumusan Isu):** Memperkenalkan perdebatan kontroversial dunia beserta dua kubu yang berselisih paham (Paragraf 1).
2. **Arguments For / Pro (Sudut Pandang Pendukung):** Uraian keunggulan, lonjakan produktivitas, dan peluang lapangan kerja baru dari penerapan isu (Paragraf 2).
3. **Arguments Against / Contra (Sudut Pandang Pengkritik):** Uraian risiko, potensi bahaya bias algoritma, dan ancaman pemutusan hubungan kerja (Paragraf 3).
4. **Balanced Conclusion (Kesimpulan Sintesis Berimbang):** Rekomendasi jalan tengah berupa kerangka regulasi dan pengawasan etis multi-pemangku kepentingan (Paragraf 4).

### 3. Contoh Teks yang Relevan (Model Text Teranotasi)
**Judul:** *The Autonomous Workplace: Collaborative Evolution or Mass Disenfranchisement?* (World Economic Forum, CEFR B2)
- **[Paragraf 1 - Statement of Issue]:** *The relentless integration of autonomous algorithmic systems and generative artificial intelligence into white-collar professions has ignited fierce international debate... economists, policymakers, and labor leaders find themselves sharply divided over whether automation represents an unprecedented emancipation of human talent or a perilous catalyst for widespread socioeconomic disenfranchisement.*
- **[Paragraf 2 - Arguments For]:** *Proponents of rapid workplace automation contend that cognitive AI elevates human productivity to historic heights. By delegating repetitive computational analysis, data verification, and drafting procedures to automated engines, professionals can redirect their cognitive energy toward high-level strategic reasoning, ethical diplomacy, and interpersonal empathy...*
- **[Paragraf 3 - Arguments Against]:** *Conversely, detractors and labor unions present grave concerns regarding structural displacement and the erosion of entry-level career pathways. When junior analyst and associate tasks are swallowed by software subscriptions, the traditional apprenticeship model through which young graduates hone professional judgment collapses...*
- **[Paragraf 4 - Balanced Conclusion]:** *In conclusion, neither blind technophilia nor reflexive resistance provides an adequate response to the fourth industrial revolution. A balanced strategy requires robust multi-stakeholder governance: corporations must reinvest productivity gains into lifelong employee reskilling, while governments formulate strict labor protections that mandate human oversight in consequential decisions...*

### 4. Grammar & Tata Bahasa Fokus
- **Contrastive Discourse Markers:**
  - *Proponents celebrate economic efficiency; **conversely**, labor unions raise alarms on structural unemployment.*
- **Academic Hedging & Reporting Verbs:**
  - *Evidence **arguably suggests** that a transitional period of frictional unemployment is inevitable.*
  - *Detractors **contend** that systemic biases remain embedded within training data.*

### 5. Pronunciation Guide & Speaking Tips
- **Disenfranchisement** (/ˌdɪsɪnˈfræntʃaɪzmənt/ - dis-en-**FRAN**-chyz-muhnt): Hilangnya hak ekonomi atau kesempatan berdaya.
- **Apprenticeship** (/əˈprentɪsʃɪp/ - uh-**PREN**-tis-ship): Masa magang dan penempaan keahlian.
- **Augmentation** (/ˌɔːɡmenˈteɪʃn/ - awg-men-**TAY**-shun): Pemberdayaan dan peningkatan kapasitas.
- **Governance** (/ˈɡʌvənəns/ - **GUV**-er-nuhns): Tata kelola regulasi yang sah dan adil.
- **Thought Grouping:** Beri jeda 1 detik (*pause*) setelah konjungsi kontras: *"Conversely, [pause] detractors emphasize that..."*.

# MATERI 4: CONDITIONAL SENTENCES TYPE 1, 2, 3 & INVERSION

### 1. Definisi Teoretis & Fungsi Komunikatif
- **Definisi:** Siswa kelas XII harus menguasai seluruh spektrum kalimat pengandaian: Type 1 (Kemungkinan masa depan), Type 2 (Khayalan bertolak belakang dengan realitas saat ini), Type 3 (Penyesalan masa lampau yang tidak mungkin terulang), serta pola Inversi Formal (tanpa kata 'If') untuk penulisan esai akademik.
- **Fungsi Sosial (*Social Function*):** *To express future possibilities, hypothetical present conditions, past counterfactual regrets, and formal stylistic inversions.*

### 2. Unsur-Unsur Struktur Teks (*Generic Structure & Text Elements*)
1. **Conditional Type 1 (Future Real):** Peluang nyata di masa depan bila syarat terpenuhi.
   - Pola: $\text{If} + \text{Simple Present (V1)}, \text{will} + \text{Verb-1}$.
   - Contoh: *If we verify data, we will avoid fake news.*
2. **Conditional Type 2 (Unreal Present):** Khayalan saat ini yang berlawanan dengan kenyataan.
   - Pola: $\text{If} + \text{Simple Past (were/V2)}, \text{would} + \text{Verb-1}$.
   - Contoh: *If he were humble, he would welcome the traveler.*
3. **Conditional Type 3 (Unreal Past / Regret):** Penyesalan masa lampau yang sudah terjadi dan tidak dapat diubah lagi.
   - Pola: $\text{If} + \text{Past Perfect (had + V3)}, \text{would have} + \text{Verb-3}$.
   - Contoh: *If the engineers had reinforced the seawall, the disaster would not have occurred.*
4. **Inversion Conditionals (Inversi Formal Tanpa 'If'):** Pembalikan letak kata kerja bantu ke depan subjek untuk gaya bahasa akademik resmi tingkat tinggi.
   - Pola: *Should you require...* (Type 1), *Were the government to mandate...* (Type 2), *Had Ki Ageng listened...* (Type 3).

### 3. Contoh Wacana yang Relevan (Model Inversi Teranotasi)
- **[Type 1 Inversion]:** *Should international inspectors visit the nuclear plant, they will observe strict safety protocols.* (Setara: If international inspectors visit...)
- **[Type 2 Inversion]:** *Were policymakers to mandate carbon taxes tomorrow, corporations would rapidly adjust their emission quotas.* (Setara: If policymakers mandated...)
- **[Type 3 Inversion]:** *Had Ki Ageng Semito embraced moral compassion, his workshop would have remained intact.* (Setara: If Ki Ageng had embraced...)
- **[Mixed Conditional]:** *If our ancestors had not preserved ancient chronicles, we would not understand our cultural identity today.*

### 4. Grammar & Tata Bahasa Fokus
- **Rumus Inversi Formal:**
  - Type 1: $\text{Should} + \text{Subject} + \text{Verb-1}, [\text{Result Clause}]$
  - Type 2: $\text{Were} + \text{Subject} + \text{to Verb-1}, [\text{Result Clause}]$
  - Type 3: $\text{Had} + \text{Subject} + \text{Verb-3}, [\text{Result Clause with would have V3}]$

### 5. Pronunciation Guide & Speaking Tips
- **Counterfactual** (/ˌkaʊntəˈfæktʃuəl/ - kown-ter-**FAK**-choo-uhl): Bertolak belakang dengan kenyataan sejarah.
- **Inversion** (/ɪnˈvɜːʃn/ - in-**VUR**-shun): Pembalikan struktur tata bahasa.
- **Requisite** (/ˈrekwɪzɪt/ - **REK**-wi-zit): Syarat yang mutlak diperlukan.
- **Solidarity** (/ˌsɒlɪˈdærəti/ - sol-i-**DARE**-i-tee): Solidaritas / kebersamaan rasa.
- **Stress pada Inversi:** Tekan kata *Had* dan kata kerja utama *listened* dengan ritme tegas: *"Had Ki Ageng listened..."*.
