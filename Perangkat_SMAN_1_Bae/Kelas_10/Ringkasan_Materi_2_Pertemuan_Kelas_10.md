# PERTEMUAN 1: DESCRIPTIVE TEXT
### *Promoting Local and National Cultural Heritage*

### 1. Tujuan Pembelajaran (TP) & Indikator (KKTP)
- **TP 10.1:** Peserta didik mampu mengidentifikasi fungsi sosial, menganalisis struktur teks, dan mengevaluasi unsur kebahasaan teks deskriptif lisan dan tulis tentang warisan budaya/alam nusantara secara kritis.
- **Indikator Ketercapaian (KKTP):**
  1. Siswa mampu menemukan ide pokok (*main idea*) dan informasi spesifik pada teks deskriptif otentik.
  2. Siswa mampu membedakan bagian *Identification* dan *Description*.
  3. Siswa mampu mengidentifikasi dan menggunakan *adjective phrases*, *sensory language*, dan *Simple Present Tense*.

### 2. Konsep Kunci Descriptive Text
| Aspek | Penjelasan Mendalam |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Social Function** | Menggambarkan secara spesifik ciri-ciri, karakteristik, dan keunikan suatu objek khusus (orang, benda, bangunan bersejarah, atau tempat wisata tertentu) agar pembaca dapat membayangkannya secara visual. |
| **Generic Structure** | **1. Identification:** Memperkenalkan objek spesifik yang akan dideskripsikan (apa/siapa, di mana lokasinya, mengapa objek tersebut istimewa).<br>**2. Description:** Menguraikan rincian fisik, bagian-bagian, fungsi, suasana, atau sifat khas objek secara sistematis dari sudut pandang indrawi. |
| **Lexicogrammatical Features** | - **Focus on Specific Participant:** Misal *Karimunjawa National Park*, *Prambanan Temple* (bukan *islands* atau *temples* secara umum).<br>- **Simple Present Tense:** Menggunakan kata kerja statis (*is, stands, features, embodies*).<br>- **Sensory Imagery & Adjectives:** Kata sifat indrawi (*crystalline turquoise water, intricate stone relief, majestic silhouette*).<br>- **Adjective Phrases:** Frasa sifat bertingkat (*a breathtakingly serene coastal sanctuary*). |

### 3. Teks Otentik (Authentic Reading Text)

> **Source:** Adapted from *Indonesia Travel & Conservation Geographic Chronicle (2025)*  
> **Title:** *Karimunjawa: The Living Coral Haven of the Java Sea*  
> **CEFR Level:** A2+ / B1  

```text
Karimunjawa Marine National Park, an archipelago of twenty-seven pristine islands situated approximately 80 kilometers northwest of Jepara in Central Java, represents one of Indonesia’s most ecologically vital maritime sanctuaries. Declared a protected marine reserve, this tranquil haven attracts both local and international researchers who seek to observe sustainable marine ecosystems harmoniously preserved by the local coastal communities.

The archipelago features breathtaking marine topography characterized by crystalline turquoise waters, vibrant fringing coral reefs, and extensive mangrove ecosystems. Beneath the calm surface, over two hundred species of scleractinian hard corals thrive alongside vibrant schools of clownfish, hawksbill sea turtles, and majestic spotted stingrays. The terrestrial landscape is equally enchanting, blanketed by verdant tropical rain forests that provide a natural habitat for the endemic red-breasted parakeet and white-bellied sea eagles.

Beyond its magnificent physical scenery, Karimunjawa offers visitors an authentic experience of community-based eco-tourism. Local fishermen on Pulau Menjangan Besar actively manage community-led shark rehabilitation pools, where wounded marine life is nurtured before being safely released into the wild. As dusk approaches, the horizon radiates an amber glow, casting long silhouettes across the white powdered sands of Tanjung Gelam Beach. The seamless integration of biological preservation and hospitable cultural traditions makes Karimunjawa a remarkable national treasure of Central Java.
```

### 5. Fokus Tata Bahasa & Kosakata Kunci

#### A. Pola Simple Present Tense pada Teks Deskriptif
Teks deskriptif menggunakan fakta permanen (*timeless facts*):
- *The archipelago **features** breathtaking marine topography.*
- *Over two hundred species of corals **thrive** beneath the surface.*
- **Formula:** 
  $$\text{Subject} + \text{Verb-1 (s/es)} + \text{Object/Complement}$$

#### B. Pembentukan Adjective Phrase (Frasa Sifat)
Adjective phrase memperkaya gambaran sensorik siswa:
- **Determiner + Adverb + Adjective + Noun:**
  - *a [Det] + breathtakingly [Adv] + serene [Adj] + sanctuary [Noun]*
  - *an [Det] + ecologically [Adv] + vital [Adj] + maritime reserve [Noun]*

#### C. Glosarium Kosakata Akademik (Glossary)
1. **Pristine** (*adj.*) /ˈprɪstiːn/: Murni, belum terjamah polusi.
2. **Archipelago** (*n.*) /ˌɑːkɪˈpeləɡəʊ/: Kepulauan.
3. **Endemic** (*adj.*) /enˈdemɪk/: Spesies asli yang hanya hidup di wilayah tertentu.
4. **Sanctuary** (*n.*) /ˈsæŋktʃuəri/: Kawasan suaka/perlindungan margasatwa.
5. **Harmoniously** (*adv.*) /hɑːˈməʊniəsli/: Secara selaras dan damai.

# PERTEMUAN 2: REPORT TEXT
### *Scientific Observation of Endemic Fauna and Environmental Phenomena*

### 1. Tujuan Pembelajaran (TP) & Indikator (KKTP)
- **TP 10.2:** Peserta didik mampu menganalisis fungsi sosial, struktur klasifikasi ilmiah, dan fitur kebahasaan Report Text mengenai fenomena alam dan konservasi satwa, serta membedakannya dengan Descriptive Text secara analitis.
- **Indikator Ketercapaian (KKTP):**
  1. Siswa mampu membedakan klasifikasi umum (*General Classification*) dan deskripsi faktual (*Description of Behaviors/Habitats*).
  2. Siswa mampu menganalisis penggunaan *Passive Voice* dan istilah teknis ilmiah (*technical vocabulary*).
  3. Siswa mampu menyimpulkan data konservasi dan pesan ilmiah dari teks observasi faktual.

### 2. Konsep Kunci Report Text
| Aspek | Penjelasan Mendalam |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Social Function** | Menyajikan informasi faktual apa adanya berdasarkan hasil pengamatan ilmiah, riset objektif, atau klasifikasi sistematis tentang sekelompok fenomena alami, sosial, atau buatan secara umum (bukan individual/spesifik). |
| **Generic Structure** | **1. General Classification:** Pernyataan umum yang mengklasifikasikan subjek ke dalam taksonomi, kategori ilmiah, atau kelompok ekologis umum.<br>**2. Description:** Menguraikan aspek-aspek umum subjek secara objektif, meliputi ciri morfologi (*physical appearance*), habitat (*natural habitat*), perilaku (*behavior*), makanan (*diet*), serta status konservasi (*conservation threats*). |
| **Lexicogrammatical Features** | - **Focus on Generic Participants:** Menggunakan kelompok umum (misal: *Rhinos, Mangrove forests, Earthquakes*), bukan objek tunggal bernama milik pribadi.<br>- **Present Generic Tense & Timeless Verbs:** Menggambarkan kebiasaan alami (*solitary mammals spend up to twelve hours...*).<br>- **Passive Voice:** Digunakan saat proses atau subjek yang diamati lebih penting daripada pelaku riset (*The species is categorized as critically endangered*).<br>- **Technical & Biological Terms:** Istilah taksonomi (*herbivorous, gestation period, poaching, ungulates*). |

### 3. Teks Otentik (Authentic Reading Text)

> **Source:** Adapted from *World Wildlife & Tropical Ecology Research Journal (2025)*  
> **Title:** *The Javan Rhinoceros (Rhinoceros sondaicus): Ecology and Survival Frontiers*  
> **CEFR Level:** B1  

```text
The Javan Rhinoceros (Rhinoceros sondaicus), also known as the lesser one-horned rhinoceros, is a critically endangered member of the family Rhinocerotidae and one of five extant rhinoceros species worldwide. Historically distributed across the lush tropical forests of Southeast Asia, Java, and Sumatra, the entire global population is now strictly confined to Ujung Kulon National Park at the westernmost tip of Java Island, Indonesia.

Morphologically, the Javan rhino possesses a dusky grey, heavily folded skin that resembles armor plating, which provides natural protection against dense tropical vegetation and thorny thickets. Unlike African rhinoceros species which develop two prominent horns, the Javan rhino typically bears a single small horn rarely exceeding twenty-five centimeters in length, often found only in males. Adults can weigh between 900 and 2,300 kilograms, yet despite their massive physical stature, they are exceptionally agile movers capable of navigating dense jungle mudflats and steep slopes.

Ecologically, Rhinoceros sondaicus is a solitary, herbivorous browser that feeds on a diverse variety of shoots, fallen fruits, twigs, and tender young leaves. They rely heavily on wallowing in natural mud pools to regulate body temperature and protect their sensitive dermal layers from parasites and insect bites. Due to severe habitat destruction and historical poaching for illegal wildlife trade, fewer than eighty individuals remain in the wild. Consequently, intensive satellite monitoring, biological habitat restoration, and anti-poaching patrols are continuously implemented to prevent the irreversible extinction of this prehistoric mammal.
```

### 5. Fokus Tata Bahasa & Kosakata Kunci

#### A. Pola Passive Voice pada Report Ilmiah
Passive voice digunakan untuk menjaga objektivitas ilmiah:
- Active: *Rangers strictly confine the population to Ujung Kulon.*
- Passive: *The entire global population **is strictly confined** to Ujung Kulon National Park.*
- **Formula:** 
  $$\text{Subject} + \text{is / am / are} + \text{Verb-3 (Past Participle)} + (\text{by Agent})$$

#### B. Glosarium Kosakata Ilmiah (Scientific Vocabulary)
1. **Extant** (*adj.*) /ˈekstənt/: Masih hidup/eksis di bumi saat ini (lawan kata dari *extinct*).
2. **Herbivorous** (*adj.*) /hɜːˈbɪvərəs/: Pemakan tumbuhan.
3. **Wallow** (*v.*) /ˈwɒləʊ/: Berkubang dalam lumpur atau air untuk mendinginkan suhu tubuh.
4. **Poaching** (*n.*) /ˈpəʊtʃɪŋ/: Perburuan satwa liar secara ilegal.
5. **Critically Endangered** (*adj.*): Berada pada ambang kepunahan sangat kritis menurut daftar merah IUCN.
